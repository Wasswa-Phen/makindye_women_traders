## 2. Mission & Vision

Makindye Women Traders is a women-led grassroots movement turning environmental responsibility into cleaner communities, stronger livelihoods, and a more hopeful Kampala.

> _“Loving nature through women ensures continuity in transformation.”_

The organisation operates on the conviction that women are not passive recipients of development aid; they are organisers, educators, earners, and community anchors capable of leading durable environmental and economic transformation.

---

## 3. Core Programmes & Flagship Initiative

### Flagship Emerging Initiative: Women-Led Recycling

Makindye Women Traders is developing a community-based recycling programme designed to turn neighbourhood waste into dignified income streams for women traders. The website transparently presents this initiative as an **emerging system under active development**—inviting technical, funding, and market partners to co-build durable local infrastructure.

### Six Core Programme Pillars

1. **Recycling & Waste Management** (`01`): Practical pathways from discarded materials to dignified livelihoods.
2. **Tree Growing & Conservation** (`02`): Restoring green spaces through community stewardship and local collaboration.
3. **Women's Empowerment & Livelihoods** (`03`): Supporting women with confidence, skills, and economic opportunity.
4. **Mentorship & Girls' Leadership** (`04`): Equipping girls to see themselves as capable leaders and environmental advocates.
5. **School Environmental Clubs** (`05`): Cultivating lifelong habits of environmental care and climate responsibility among students.
6. **Community Outreach** (`06`): Taking practical environmental education to communities across Kampala and Northern Uganda.

### Key Collaborators

- **Kaazi Tree Grow Project**
- **Sanctuary Church Gulu**

## 4. Target Audiences

1. **Development & Funding Partners**: Grants, philanthropic foundations, environmental NGOs, and CSR sponsors seeking credible grassroots women-led partners in Uganda.
2. **Technical & Market Partners**: Waste management specialists, recycling processors, and circular-economy enterprises.
3. **Community Members & Volunteers**: Local residents, educators, school leaders, and youth volunteers in Kampala and partner districts.
